Operational Capabilities

Comprehensive modules for end-to-end airport management

✈️

Flight Operations

Manage seasonal and daily schedules with SSIM import support. Track real-time status (Off-Block, Airborne, Landed) and manage delays efficiently.

📊

Resource Allocation

Intelligent, constraint-based assignment for Gates, Stands, and Check-in counters. Visual Gantt charts for conflict detection and timeline management.

📺

Real-time FIDS

WebSocket-driven Flight Information Display System. Push updates instantly to passenger screens without page reloads.

💰

Aeronautical Billing

Automated fee calculation engine for landing, parking, and passenger charges. Custom rate cards and invoicing support.

📂

Master Data

Complete "Digital Twin" modeling of your airport infrastructure: Terminals, Runways, Aircraft types, and Airlines.

📈

BI Analytics

Deep insights into On-Time Performance (OTP) and resource utilization using high-performance time-series data analysis.
