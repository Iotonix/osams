About This View

This GANTT chart displays a typical day view of seasonal flight schedules. Select a day of week to see which flights operate on that day. Gate/stand occupation is shown for 2 hours (arrivals from landing, departures ending at takeoff). Baggage carousels activate 30 minutes after landing for 45 minutes.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Shift + Drag: Pan timeline
  • Click group header: Expand/collapse resources
